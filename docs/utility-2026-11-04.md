## Limits and next work

Dimensions are original estimated proportions, not manufacturer measurements. Washer loading access and cabinet sliding are two discrete poses, not continuous animation. Eight new assets are explicitly static: no wash cycle, rotating drum, humidity change, powered device simulation, cloth draping, hook attachment, wheel motion, adjustable board or folding rack. The dehumidifier is a static appliance depiction, not an operating machine. Step/load/ergonomic safety is not certified. Clearance covers authored endpoints, not swept motion. SVG/BSP illustration has no physical solver, shadows or GLB export; the scene is an unenclosed utility arrangement without architectural walls or certified circulation. No missing item is hidden by counting parts, existing props or the assembly as new assets.

Next priorities: living/waiting-room soft seating and floor lighting; independently reusable architectural/installed fixtures with attachment contracts; richer appliance and textile behaviors.
