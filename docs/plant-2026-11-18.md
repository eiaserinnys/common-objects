## Selection and scope

The existing collection covered everyday appliances, customer-facing equipment, workshops and lobbies, but lacked dedicated building water, air and electrical plant. Ten new assets add air handling, hot water storage, booster pumping, expansion buffering, softening, electrical distribution, air dampers, plate heat exchange, condensate lifting and cable routing. They are not generic cabinet recolors or replacements for the existing dehumidifier, purifier, radiator or air compressor. No new small hand-tool assortment.

All are original **3D-coordinate primitive geometry projected into SVG**, not delivered GLB/mesh exports. Seven sources have role-specific discrete controls or guarded process illustrations; three are fixed depictions. Individual pumps, vessels in the softener, exchanger plates and retained sample cables remain internal components and do not inflate the independent count.

Existing wall panel (two instances), sorting table, study lamp, ruler and mechanic stool are **five reused source IDs/six reused instances**. Combined scene: **16 instances**, two wall mounts and two horizontal support attachments. The table carries lamp/ruler at .92 m. Mounted panel/ladder follow independent host walls. There is no fully enclosed room, inter-equipment pipe/wire network, certified service aisle or actual engineering system.
