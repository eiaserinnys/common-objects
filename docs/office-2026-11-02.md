## Limits

3D-coordinate SVG projection, not delivered GLB meshes. BSP painter rendering has no physics, shadows or depth buffer. Discrete pose switching is not continuous animation; scanner/cassette remain static and printing is only a paper-path illustration. Furniture carries no load/tip certification. Supports are hints: moving a parent does not automatically carry placed objects. Empty archive shelves and absent walls are deliberate scope limits; no new architectural room is counted. Estimates are original nominal designs, not measured branded products. Existing asset versions were untouched. No repository CI workflow is configured; local passing checks are not a CI pass.
