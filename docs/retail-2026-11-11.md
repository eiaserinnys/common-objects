## Why retail now

Existing café selling, bakery processing, home storage and office shelves did not supply grocery merchandising, customer shopping transport, weighing/labeling and checkout flow. Yesterday's architecture supplies reusable installation context; repeating plain walls would add less role coverage. This set fills those retail roles and introduces explicit operation prerequisites rather than another access-door-only machine batch. Enclosure joining and attachment authoring remain future priorities.

There are now **215 registrations: 196 independent sources (104 S / 29 M / 63 L), 18 imported compound groups and one original room**. New 0 S / 4 M / 6 L improves the independent small share **55.9% → 53.1%**. The trolley is medium because its largest actual extent is .983 m, despite its transport role. ## Guarded operations and boundaries

The shared model accepts optional Boolean action prerequisites, atomic transition assignments and conditional state rules; [specification](asset-spec.md#guarded-discrete-operations-2026-11-11). Sources own operation rules, the model executes them and the preview retains its existing atomic attachment-resolution/rollback workflow. Old asset sources and immutable versions are unchanged.

Conveyor advance refuses while stopped or already delivered; reload refuses while live. Register clearing refuses with drawer open; an open sale drawer requires its printed receipt. Printing a scale label requires a weighed sample, including on restored scenes. Bulk dispensing refuses with refill lid open; refill requires the open lid. Every allowed and denied action from all reachable states is checked; rejected actions preserve the entire scene. Browser tests exercise six representative refusals and visible status messages.

These are synchronous discrete illustrations, **not elapsed-time machines**. The retained sample parcel, bag, receipt and fill demonstrate behavior; there are no real payments, calibrated weights, barcode content, inventories, sensors, physical cooling, motor motion, granular flow or certified safety controls. The dry-goods hopper is shown with its front open as a cutaway to expose fill levels, not a sealed food container. The night screen is opaque. Support/mount rules remain yaw-only, unit-scale, authored JSON; no automatic collision prevention, moving-belt transport of arbitrary children, interactive binding editor or swept dynamics. SVG/BSP remains authoritative, with no GLB export, transparency or cast lighting.
