## Limits and unfinished capabilities

SVG painter rendering has no WebGL depth buffer, cast shadows or transparency model. No physics, patient model, healthcare assessment, real measurements, resistance, hydraulic force, load certification, wheel rolling or elapsed exercise cycle is implemented. Nine new objects have explicit discrete operation; the staircase is static. The wheel's outer track is fixed while its spokes/handle turn. Stadiometer marks are illustrative and do not produce a numeric reading. The open arrangement is not a complete enclosed room. Existing reused props keep their original limitations.
