## Reproducible actual-preview checks

Serve `dist` after build and open `/preview/salon.html`. The retained Chromium script uses the real controls:

1. Drive each of the 19 poses by its recorded button sequence, then drag the camera through four directions. Inspect body retention, open cavities, swivel hierarchy and occlusion. No state is created by replacing the renderer with a mock.
2. Try water before reclining, dryer power before lowering, extraction with filter out, warming with door open and curing with tray extended. Also try reversing each required access/position while operating. All ten refusals must leave the exact saved scene unchanged. Invalid saved Boolean combinations are separately rejected by scene-load tests.
3. Duplicate every one of the 15 instances; independently operate copies. Copies detach, keep unit scale and preserve original states. Save, reload and restore all 30 exact transforms/states.
4. Reload initial scene. Move hosts 4 (manicure table), 7 (credenza) and 9 (cart) X +.12 m and yaw 20°. Raise/swivel chair 1, recline and run wash 2, lower and power dryer 3, run table 4, warm cabinet 5, insert and cure hood 6, brake cart 9. Check five child heights and inherited cabinet yaw 110°. Braked cart transform fields must disable. Save/reload/restore the whole combined state exactly.
5. After restoration, reject hood tray extension while curing. Duplicate hood 6 and stop/extend only the detached copy; original remains operating and attached. Detach basket 10 without a world-position jump, unbrake cart and move cart X to 3.1 m; detached basket must stay. A second exact save/reload/restore verifies these combined changes.
6. At 390 px viewport, inspect overflow, selection, focus, camera drag, zoom and primary action.

A plausible implementation that toggles only a seat but leaves arms/footrest behind fails the retained hierarchy test. A support relation that follows only translation fails yaw/height checks; a copy sharing state fails original equality; saving state without attachments fails exact reload; guard errors mutating earlier fields fail whole-scene equality. Initial mixed endpoint pairs and the combined rotated scene receive conservative per-part bounds checks (combined check uses a common inverse-yaw frame to avoid spurious parent/child world-AABB intersections). These are not a continuous collision solver; arbitrary edits can intersect.

Initial contact checks found tiny gaps in the basin pedestal/tap mounting, control lamps, motor bridge and nail-hood shell. Those were corrected before final tests and rendering. No test was weakened to ignore disconnected parts.
