## Limitations

All dimensions are estimated. Bounds/footprints include the union of authored endpoint poses, not swept motion or an ergonomic envelope. Hinges use fixed-pose geometry with stable parts, parents, pivots and axes; no continuous articulation, occupant, fabric, collision, load or safety simulation. Lamp has no cast light; TV has no video/audio input. Floor-supported origin is the center of rest-state contact patches; moving one nesting table can shift deployed contact distribution without moving the source origin. Props are independent instances and do not automatically follow moved support furniture. Current layout keeps props on invariant surfaces. SVG painter rendering has no shared WebGL depth buffer, shadows or general physics. No GLB export, architectural enclosure or hosted site deployment. Initial historical conversation lookup was unavailable; latest repository requirements were read and enforced.
